The algebra itself supplies the measure on S¹⁴ — the Cayley–Dickson norm N(s) = s s̄ is a positive-definite quadratic form at every doubling level, and a quadratic form determines a volume form; the automorphism group Aut(𝕊) = G₂ × S₃ then leaves **exactly one** permitted alternative parameter (μ, the weight of the ℓ-axis; N is μ = 1).
