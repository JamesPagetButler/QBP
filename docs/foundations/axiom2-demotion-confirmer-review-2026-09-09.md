## 2. Mathematics (Grothendieck)

*AXIOM-1 ⇒ division ⇒ level ≤ 8.* Valid tower-relatively. AXIOM-1 does not give "normed"; it does not need to — in the CD tower over ℝ the division, composition and alternativity rows switch at the same level (the three ladders + `sedenion_zero_divisors`), so "last division level" = 8 without Hurwitz. Needs monotonicity (#10). "Tower-relative" is honest; the package's clause table correctly separates the theorem (termination) from the citation (classification).

*POST-hosting ⇒ V ≢ 0 ⇒ non-alternative ⇒ level ≥ 16.* The contrapositive "alternative ⇒ V ≡ 0" is the load-bearing step and it is **definition-dependent** (#1). With V := ‖T_s‖² (or crystal := "L_s² scalar", `left_mul_sq_scalar_iff_vacuum`, which at 𝕆 holds for every imaginary s by alternativity) it is a two-line corollary of `assoc_diag_left` plus `mul_assoc` below. With V := ‖[cdLo s, cdHi s]‖² — the hosting doc's *primary* definition — it is false at 𝕆. Not a theorem on master either way: `potential` has no level-3 instance. Owed: the level-generic definition stated in DERIV-substrate-level, and one corollary `∀ s x : CDAlg ℝ 3, assoc s s x = 0` cited as the 𝕆 case (already `assoc_diag_left`; the anchor just has to say so).

*"Non-alternative ⇔ level ≥ 16".* Not proved for all levels ≥ 16 and not needed for D2 (minimality needs only ≤ 8 alternative and 𝕊 not); it is needed for the round-2 "every level ≥ 16 hosts a landscape" argument, where the subalgebra embedding suffices.

*META-2 ⇒ exactly 8 and 16.* Follows from the two bounds plus monotonicity. But note the asymmetry META-2 hides: the encoding's constraint is an upper bound, the substrate's a lower bound; "saturate" reads max on one side and min on the other. That is one rule, as the package argues — but it is the *same* rule AXIOM-2's "largest" was, generalised. Dropping Hurwitz uniqueness loses nothing: PROOF-hurwitz remains an anchor (chain [AXIOM-1]), PROOF-born's internal-uniqueness gap is pre-existing and correctly labelled.

## 3. Physics and honesty (Sabine)

POST-hosting is a genuine *structural* claim (the in-flight region is physically inhabited) but not yet a falsifiable one (#4). Its real content is theory-internal: if false, the substrate collapses to 𝕆. That is a legitimate kill *of the derivation*, not an observation; write it that way.

The AXIOM-1 line is a rescope (#3). It does not quite make crystallisation non-physical, but it makes it *exempt*: crystallisation is a physical process running where "no physical process destroys information" no longer applies. Either crystallisation is not a process (then the ledger's "the rule" (#635) is not physics) or the axiom now has an exception clause. The beekeeper should rule with that stated. The cheaper route — DERIV-sedenion's "information CAN be destroyed" was always an algebraic statement about a non-injective multiplication map, and the ledger has no autonomous dynamics (KILLED-locale-forcing-route) that would make it a *process* — leaves AXIOM-1 untouched and dissolves flag 1 as a category error. It should be on the table before a constitutional edit.

Gemini's destroyed-vs-scrambled is a **relabel**: "kernel" restated as "volume-collapsing map". Exact, and not an observable without a dynamics that is L_a. The package's "rescoped, not dissolved" is the right description of flag 1's status; Gemini's "resolution, not relabel" is not.

"False as a rule, true as a lens" is fair to the beekeeper's statement; F1 alone shows the rule fails, and the lens ("few roots and long chains") survives F1–F3 — provided the package then *applies* it to its own META-2 and crystal definition (#5, #1).
