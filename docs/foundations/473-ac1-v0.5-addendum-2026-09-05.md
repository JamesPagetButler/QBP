## 2. What survived, sharpened

**Prop 16, three layers.** Lean coverage, stated exactly (`NoAutonomousDynamics.lean` docstring): layer 1 is in Lean; of layer 2 only the invariant-subspace half is in Lean (every word in {1, s, ℓ} lies in ℍ_s) — "only ±ℓ reachable" rests on the numerical shape invariant (`lmaps_check.py`); layer 3 is numerical (`generic_maps_check.py`, five independent t; ~5 min) with the mechanism below:

| Generators | Generated structure | Algebraic maps | Reachable vacua |
|---|---|---|---|
| one imaginary s | span{1, s} | s ↦ ±s | none new |
| s and the forced ℓ | quaternion subalgebra ℍ_s (S³, an H-space) | finite-order symmetries; (s+ℓ)/‖·‖ runs to the pole | ±ℓ only |
| s and an unforced generic t | **all of 𝕊** (dim 16 by word length 5; exact for an integer witness, hence for all generic pairs — confirmer) | x·t, t·x, [x, t]: **power iteration** of the linear skew maps R_t, L_t, ad_t — a transient onto the top-singular plane, which lies on the **zero-divisor ridge V = 1**, then a period-2 symmetry; (x+t)/‖·‖ attracts to t; x·(x·t) leaves Im𝕊 | none: the only non-symmetric behaviour is a linear transient onto the zero-divisor locus, never toward the vacuum |

**Mechanism (confirmer, verified):** Σσ²(R_t) = 16·N(t) exactly (each e_k·t has norm N(t)), so σ_max > 1 ⇔ σ_min < 1: a dominant plane exists *because* the norm is not multiplicative (Prop 7′). For ℓ, R_ℓ is orthogonal (Lean `N_ell_mul`), so no dominant plane and no transient — which is why the forced pair gives symmetries only. That the top plane lies on the ZD ridge is observed (100/100 t; V = 1 points are exact zero divisors), not derived; open: an identity for N(xt) − N(x)N(t) placing the maximiser on the ZD locus.

Reading: **no autonomous algebraic dynamics toward the vacuum.** The algebra's operations give symmetries, the ℓ-axis pole, and — with an unforced element — a linear transient onto the zero-divisor locus that is itself a consequence of the norm's failure to compose. Motion toward the vacuum sphere (away from its poles) needs calculus (a gradient, hence a metric) or data beyond the algebra. Kill condition (restated — the old one fired trivially on (x+t)/‖·‖ with t chosen on the vacuum manifold): a *multiplicative* (x, t)-word, t generic, whose iterates converge to a vacuum with |b₀| < 1.

**T-A, stated with directions.** Existence: composition algebras in dims 1, 2, 4, 8 (Lean, PROOF-normed-division-tower-existence); H-space on S³ (Agda, S3FromCD). Non-existence: no composition algebra at 16 (Hurwitz, cited); no H-space on S¹⁵ (Adams, **not in the ledger**). Prop 7′'s "what breaks at 16" is the fact both non-existence results witness. Proposed anchor: REF-adams-hopf-invariant-one.

## 3. Is AC2 the right question? Proposal AC2″ (for the beekeeper; not adopted here)

AC2 as written asks for *the single* prediction and conflates the route with the stack. The MDL gate behind it is right; the shape is wrong. Proposed:

> **AC2″ (revised after the confirmer).** The substrate earns in iff it fixes a measured, **ℝ-non-trivial** number (one that ℝ + doubling leaves free) whose value does not move under any choice inside the PERMITTED classes it declares — (i) the admissible class of initial measures, (ii) the dynamical rule, (iii) the b₀ → observable map — those classes having been fixed before the number was looked at. "Differs from the Haar baseline 1/15" is *not* a criterion (quench 0.146, anneal ≈ 1/3 and the ℓ-map 1 all differ from it; that null only says "some dynamics"). The real null is invariance under the declared classes; naming a knob PERMITTED with a kill condition does not make it cheaper in MDL terms, and choosing the class after the data launders it. Under this form the baryon-additivity theorem is excluded on principle (degree is additive under *any* H-space multiplication — Eckmann–Hilton — so it is ℝ-trivial), not by the shape of the test.

Consistency note: the ledger's DERIV-crystallisation-asymptotic already models constants as asymptotic, never frozen — a distributional stance that AC2's "single prediction" sits awkwardly against.

## 7. Beekeeper ruling (2026-09-05): the three-horn crux — horn 1 adopted

Recorded on #473. The crux was reframed before the ruling: the three horns are all maximum-entropy distributions, differing only in the constraint set admitted; the algebra supplies the norm N and nothing else that constrains the ensemble (Props 7′, 9, 16), so maximum entropy under the algebra's own constraints *is* N's surface measure. **Ruling: horn 1, by adopting the maximum-entropy principle relative to the algebra's constraints as a PERMITTED postulate** (a principle of inference, not a theorem about sedenions). Q_μ (μ ≠ 1) and the thermal family (β_init ≠ 0) are excluded as requiring data the algebra does not supply; they remain hypotheses to test against. Consequence: "the algebra's number, given a rule" is well-defined (quench 0.146; anneal ≈ 1/3; ℓ-axis 1); the residual is the rule (#635) and the b₀ → observable map (#637). Kill condition: a b₀-tied measurement inconsistent with the horn-1 prediction under the declared rule. In the proposed CHAIN-substrate-stack-to-b0 the measure-class step is now ruled, so the weakest link is the rule postulate. Prop 12 ratification and the AC1 re-scope sentence remain open.
