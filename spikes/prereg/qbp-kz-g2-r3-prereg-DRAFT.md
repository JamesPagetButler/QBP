## 1. Question

The two ramp rounds (QBP#793, PR #796) read **H-first by the frozen ramp rule (a)**: the hysteresis gap does not close over τ_Q = 1600–12800 (0.5171 → 0.4769, OLS slope −0.039 ± 0.009). A **post-hoc, not pre-registered** look at the same raw files (QBP#797 table) shows the two branches' drop temperatures converging from both sides — cooling drop 0.977 → 1.094, heating drop 1.198 → 1.172 — with the loop width falling 0.221 → 0.149 → 0.104 → 0.078 in T over τ_Q 1600 → 12800, ≈ τ_Q^−0.5, the branches converging toward T ≈ 1.15. That trend is consistent with **both** a first-order transition under a finite-rate sweep (nucleation closer to coexistence when given more time) **and** a continuous one (a finite-rate lag that closes as τ_Q → ∞); no further ramp bracket decides it. The decisive test is the **equilibrium** one, no ramp, at fixed T, as a function of L.

- **H-FO (first-order):** at some T in the window the equilibrium energy-density distribution is **double-peaked**, and the **free-energy barrier between the peaks, ΔF_L = ln(h_peak,lower / h_valley), does not shrink as L grows** (Lee–Kosterlitz: for a first-order transition the barrier is an interface cost and grows with L^{d−1}; for a continuous transition or a crossover any apparent two-peak structure flattens with L). The energy Binder cumulant V_L = 1 − ⟨e⁴⟩ / (3 ⟨e²⟩²) is reported as a **secondary**, not a gate (r3 note below).
- **H-CONT (continuous):** the energy-density distribution is **single-peaked at every T** at L = 12 and L = 16 (no barrier at any T), narrowing with L.

*r3 note (director, Red Team on r2, pre-data).* r2 gated on V_min(L) < 2/3 − 0.02. With e measured from the chordal zero (perfect alignment), e near T_c is O(1) and the cumulant's depth below 2/3 is ≈ (e₁² − e₂²)² / (12 e₁² e₂²) for an equal-weight two-state distribution, i.e. second order in the latent heat over the mean energy: a two-state system with Δe ≈ 0.2 on e ≈ 1 sits only ≈ 0.01 below 2/3 and would have failed (a), while every finite sample sits slightly below 2/3 anyway (≈ (4/3) σ²/μ² for a single narrow peak), so "< 2/3" alone is vacuous. The histogram barrier is the direct, shift-independent statistic and is what §3 now gates on; V_L and its 1/L³ extrapolation are reported beside it.

**Pre-data prior: H-FO favoured, ≈ 75 %.** Two grounds, both already on file: (i) the order parameter is the projector Π onto the 3-plane, a traceless symmetric tensor carrying a Landau cubic invariant tr(Π_tl³), the nematic Q-tensor class whose ordering transitions are first-order by that mechanism (R1-FROZEN §1; inter#249 wisdom); (ii) the two ramp rounds (P1 hit at ≈ 70 %). **The prior does not enter the rule.** §3 is written so that H-CONT and inconclusive are each reachable from data alone, and the notary's binding condition is that this be checkable on the frozen text.

**What "equilibrium" means here (scope, not a claim) [drafter's note for the director].** The sampler is the same Model-A step rule as the ramp rounds (FROZEN-v1 §2a: synchronous Stiefel gradient step with η = 0.1 plus Gaussian noise of variance 2ηT, Gram–Schmidt), run at **fixed T** long enough to forget the random start. Its stationary distribution is the stationary distribution of that discrete dynamics at η = 0.1, which approximates the Boltzmann distribution of the chordal energy with an O(η) discretisation error; this round does **not** re-tune η and does **not** claim the exact Boltzmann ensemble. Every outcome row in §3 is a statement about "this model, this energy, this sampler (Model A, η = 0.1)", matching the claim scope of the ramp rounds.

Not asked here: the topology of G₂/SO(4), any KZ exponent, any defect count, the S³ null, the −φ² landscape, M1–M3, any CD speed claim, any value of T_c beyond the window, any critical exponent under H-CONT.

## 2. Design (equilibrium sampling at fixed T) — all numbers [proposal]

**What runs.** Equilibrium Model-A sampling at fixed T on a T grid, at three lattice sizes, several independent seeds per (L, T). No ramp, no hold-then-cool, no hysteresis metric, no detector, no ladder, no null arm, no Stage 0a re-run. T_c is **not** re-derived (round 1's value is used only to place one grid point).

**T grid [r4; supersedes the r1 grid].** A frozen table of **ten T values, indexed t ∈ 0..9 in ascending T: {1.08, 1.10, 1.12, 1.13, 1.14, 1.15, 1.16, 1.17, 1.18, 1.20}** (units of J): the coarse 0.02 grid from 1.08 to 1.20 bracketing the converging drop temperatures at τ_Q = 12800 (1.094 cooling, 1.172 heating), plus a **0.01 refinement on 1.12–1.18** where the barrier maximum at the larger lattices should sit (Gemini r1, Feynman 2: a 0.02 step can straddle the two-state window at L = 16). **L = 12 and L = 16 run all ten points; L = 8 runs the seven coarse points** t ∈ {0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 9} (the refinement is wasted at L = 8, whose window is wide). Round 1's T_c = 1.0666 is not on the grid (both ramp branches now sit above it; r2 decision). The T values are typed into the harness as a frozen table, not computed.

**Lattices.** **L ∈ {8, 12, 16}**, periodic, indexed l ∈ {0: 8, 1: 12, 2: 16}. L = 16 is the ramp rounds' size; 8 and 12 give the finite-size direction. (No L = 24: at L³ scaling it would cost 3.4× an L = 16 process; see §5.)

**Per run (one seed at one (L, T)) [proposal; r5 start rule].** Start state: for replicates **j = 0 and j = 1, i.i.d. Haar-G₂ planes** seeded by the run seed (FROZEN-v1 §2a, as implemented in `NewKZPlaneFieldHaar`) — the **hot** start; for replicate **j = 2, the fully aligned field** (every site the same plane, the chordal-energy zero; its noise stream still seeded by the run seed) — the **cold** start, built by `NewKZPlaneFieldAligned(L, seed)`: one Haar plane drawn from the seed at every site; G₂-invariance of E and of S makes which plane immaterial (director's ruling, r6). The hot/cold pair is the standard metastability probe (notary r2/r3 concern): if a second phase exists and the hot seeds never nucleate it (or the cold seed never melts) the two starts disagree, which §3 reads as **start dependence**; under a genuinely continuous transition both starts converge to the same stationary distribution. Then **burn-in 5,000 plane-steps** at T, discarded; then **50,000 sampling steps** at T; **S and the energy density e are recorded after every 10th step** of the sampling phase — after sampling steps 10, 20, …, 50,000, **no step-0 record** (director's ruling, r6) → **exactly 5,000 (S, e) samples per seed**. The burn-in is not recorded (director's r2 decision: nothing in §3 reads it; the equilibration check is the per-run τ_int and the blocked errors below). **Energy density** e = E / (3 L³), the chordal bond energy Σ_bonds (3 − ‖F_a F_bᵀ‖_F²) per bond (J = 1; e ∈ [0, 3]; zero at perfect alignment), as computed by `KZPlaneField.Energy()` divided by the bond count. The Binder cumulant is scale-invariant in e but not shift-invariant, so the energy zero is fixed here: the chordal energy's own zero, no subtraction.

**Seeds per (L, T).** **3 independent seeds, j = 0, 1, 2**, each its own noise stream; j = 0, 1 hot (Haar) starts, j = 2 the cold (aligned) start (r5 start rule above). Each (L, T) point pools its seeds' samples for the histogram and the barrier (§3) and also reports them per seed. The round runs **3 seeds per point, the r4 T table (10 points at L = 12 and 16, 7 at L = 8), 27 processes, 15,000 samples per point** (sized in §5).

**Processes.** **One driver process per (L, T)**, running its 3 seeds (j = 0, 1, 2) **sequentially**, so each process is independently capped (2 h wall, pause file, RSS, output — §5). **27 processes** (10 + 10 + 7); they run **one at a time**. **Run order (director's ruling, r6):** L = 8 (t ascending) → L = 12 (t ascending) → L = 16 (t ascending); a sequential shell loop over the 27 (L, t) pairs calling the wrapper is allowed; never two processes at once. Each process writes `spikes/qbp-kz-g2/raw/qbp-kz-g2-r3-eq-L<L>-t<t>.jsonl` (t = the T index, two digits) with a meta sidecar, and its sha256 is posted with the others before any analysis (§7). **Autocorrelation [proposal; units fixed at r6].** For each run the integrated autocorrelation time of e is estimated from the recorded series by the standard windowed sum — **Sokal window: W = the smallest W with W ≥ 6·τ(W)**, τ(W) = ½ + Σ_{k=1}^{W} ρ(k) in record units (director's ruling, r6) — and written as `tau_int_rec` (in RECORD units) with **n_eff = 5,000 / (2·tau_int_rec)**, identically 50,000 / (2·τ_steps) with τ_steps = 10·tau_int_rec (τ_int in sampling steps); the analyzer also reports the blocked standard error of ⟨e⟩ at block sizes 10, 50, 250 records. **Under-sampling flag:** a run with n_eff < 20 (τ_int > 1,250 steps, i.e. tau_int_rec > 125) is flagged `undersampled`; §3 says what the flag does.

## 3. Decision rule (pre-data; to be frozen verbatim) — thresholds [proposal]

**Statistics.** For each L, pool the samples of all T and all seeds at that L and set **60 equal-width bins over that pooled min–max of e** (common bins across T at a given L, so the histograms at different T are comparable) [proposal]. For each (L, T): the histogram h_{L,T}(e) of the 3 seeds' 5,000 samples each (15,000 samples), smoothed by a 3-bin moving average for the peak/valley search only.

**Bimodality and the barrier [proposal; r3, noise floor and scatter added at r4 (Gemini r1)].** The smoothed histogram at (L, T) has a **candidate** two-peak structure iff it has **two local maxima** (each a bin strictly above both neighbours; if more than two, the pair is chosen by the r6 analyzer-precision rule below) with at least one bin between them; h_valley is the minimum of the smoothed histogram between them (smoothed-count units). The candidate is **resolved** — and only then does the histogram count as **bimodal** — iff the valley is deeper than counting noise: **n_lower-peak − n_valley > 3 · √(n_lower-peak + n_valley)** on the smoothed counts (a 3σ Poisson floor, so a one-bin ripple in a single peak never reads as a barrier). Its **barrier** is **ΔF_L(T) = ln( h_lower-peak / h_valley )**; an unresolved or unimodal histogram has **ΔF_L(T) = 0** by definition. **Primary statistic:** **ΔF*(L) = max over the T grid of ΔF_L(T)**, with T*(L) the T at which it occurs (the first T in ascending order if tied). **Seed scatter:** at every (L, T) the barrier is also computed on each leave-one-seed-out subsample; **σ_ΔF(L, T)** is the jackknife standard error of ΔF_L(T) from those; **σ*(L) = σ_ΔF at T*(L)**.

**Analyzer precision (director's ruling, r6).** "Complete set" = exactly 27 files, one per (L, t) of §2, each `Complete` (meta + eq + end, no abort), the same `prereg_sha`, mode `eq` (never `smoke-eq`). The 3-bin smoothing at the two edge bins averages the available neighbours (truncated window). The candidate pair is the pair of strict local maxima ≥ 1 bin apart with the largest resolved barrier. All counts in the 3σ test and in ΔF are the smoothed counts; if h_valley = 0 after smoothing it is set to 0.5 (half a count) before the log. The jackknife subsamples use the fixed 60-bin grid of the full pooled sample at that L. The hot pair's blocked SE (start-dependence clause below) uses blocks that never straddle the two seeds (40 blocks of 250 records; the cold seed alone, 20 blocks). The secondaries "at (16, T*(16))" are reported only if ΔF*(16) > 0; when every ΔF_16(T) = 0, T*(16) is the lowest grid T by the tiebreak only and carries no physical meaning.

*Note for the notary (director, r6; flagged, not a rule change).* With 3 seeds a leave-one-seed-out subsample (10,000 samples) may fail the 3σ floor, making σ_ΔF large and the L-trend clauses of (a) loose — by design, conservative toward (c), never toward a false (b). And the 3σ floor applied to 3-bin-smoothed counts over-states the Poisson noise by ≈ √3 — deliberately conservative.

**Rule [proposal; r4], with the thresholds ΔF₀ = ln 2 (valley at most half the lower peak) and the L-trend tolerance ε(L₁, L₂) = max( 0.2, 2 · √(σ*(L₁)² + σ*(L₂)²) ) (never below 0.2 in ln units, and never below twice the measured seed scatter of the two barriers compared):**

- **(a) H-FO** iff **ΔF*(16) ≥ ΔF₀** **and** **ΔF*(16) ≥ ΔF*(12) − ε(16, 12)** **and** **ΔF*(12) ≥ ΔF*(8) − ε(12, 8)** (the barrier is present at the largest lattice and does not shrink with L beyond noise; ΔF*(8) may be 0).
- **(b) H-CONT** iff **ΔF_16(T) = 0 and ΔF_12(T) = 0 at every T** — i.e. no *resolved* two-peak structure at any T at the two larger lattices; with the 3σ floor above, a single-peak ripple cannot defeat (b) (Gemini r1, Feynman 1).
- **(c) inconclusive** otherwise (e.g. a barrier at L = 16 that is smaller than at L = 12 by more than ε; a barrier below ΔF₀ at L = 16; a resolved two-peak structure at L = 12 only).

**Precedence: (a), then (b), then (c).** (a) and (b) are mutually exclusive (a barrier ≥ ln 2 at L = 16 is not ΔF_16 = 0), so the order matters only for the record. The rule is total: every data set lands in exactly one row.

**Under-sampling sub-clause [r5; symmetric, per notary r2/r3].** Two gates, one per direction:
- **(a)-side:** if any of the 3 runs at (16, T*(16)) is flagged `undersampled` (§2: n_eff < 20), rule (a) **cannot fire** (a phase-locked seed must not be read as a deep valley).
- **(b)-side:** if **any run at any (L ∈ {12, 16}, T)** is flagged `undersampled`, rule (b) **cannot fire** — (b) asserts unimodality at every T, so every one of those runs must be adequately sampled; an under-sampled T may be hiding a barrier (notary: the all-unimodal case has no meaningful T*, so the check is at every T, not at T*).
In either case the round reads **(c) inconclusive (sampling)** with the flagged τ_int values reported; a longer-sampling round is a new pre-registration. Flags at L = 8 are reported and do not gate.

**Start-dependence clause [r5; the metastability guard].** At every (L ∈ {12, 16}, T), with the hot seeds j ∈ {0, 1} pooled and the cold seed j = 2 alone, compute **D_e = |⟨e⟩_cold − ⟨e⟩_hot| / √(SE_cold² + SE_hot²)** and **D_S** likewise for S, the SEs being the blocked standard errors at block size 250 records (§2). The point is **start-dependent** iff **max(D_e, D_S) > 3**. **(b) H-CONT additionally requires no start-dependent point at any (L ∈ {12, 16}, T).** A start-dependent point whose pooled (hot + cold) histogram is a *resolved* two-peak structure contributes to ΔF_L(T) exactly as any other (the barrier between the starts' phases is real two-state evidence, and (a) proceeds on the ΔF clauses); a start-dependent point **without** a resolved two-peak structure cannot support (b) and, if the ΔF clauses do not give (a), the round reads **(c) inconclusive (metastability)**. This closes the path by which seeds locked in one phase would read as "continuous" (notary r3: a hidden second phase → unimodal → ΔF = 0 → false (b)).

**Sub-labels of (c) (director's ruling, r6).** When (b) fails on both gates (an under-sampled run and a start-dependent point) the round reads **"(c) inconclusive (sampling)"** — sampling takes precedence over metastability. The round reads **"(c) inconclusive (metastability)"** iff (a) does not fire and at least one start-dependent point (L ∈ {12, 16}) has no resolved two-peak structure. Plain **"(c) inconclusive"** otherwise.

**Binder cumulant (secondary, non-gating; r3).** V_L(T) = 1 − ⟨e⁴⟩ / (3 ⟨e²⟩²) over the pooled samples at (L, T), V_min(L) = min over T, and the two-point extrapolation V* from V_min(12) and V_min(16) in 1/L³ are reported beside the barrier, with the note of §1 on their shift-dependence; they decide nothing.
