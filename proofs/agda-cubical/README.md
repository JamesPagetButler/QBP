### Performance discipline (SkyrmionCharge.agda)

Concrete-index instantiations of `Cubical.Homotopy.Group.PinSn` lemmas
(`πₙ'Sⁿ≅ℤ 2`, …) require `--lossy-unification` (as PinSn itself uses), and
their contract statements must be spelled **verbatim** in the library's form —
routing them through local aliases makes conversion normalize the full iso
(observed >30 min / 6 GB before kill). Degree-based statements (`degree 3`)
are insensitive to this. Projecting `μ`/`μₗ` out of the transported
`S³-HSpace` and *normalizing* them (e.g. computing "B(μ 1 ·) = 1" by brute
force) diverges (>8 GB) — that route stays forbidden. The theorem itself is
now proved in `SubstrateCharge.agda` via the module-abstraction barrier:
all reasoning happens over a *generic* `H : HSpace (S₊∙ 3)` whose μ is a
neutral variable, and `S³-HSpace` is substituted only by application, which
the checker never normalizes (~10 s / <700 MB, vs the >8 GB divergence).

## Convention note (#578 AC5)

This port uses **Baez's** CD convention `(a,b)(c,d) = (ac − db*, a*d + cb)`
(faithful to BR). The Lean #474 foundation (`proofs/QBP/Foundations/CDAlg.lean`)
uses **Schafer's** `(ac − d̄b, da + bc̄)`. Both standard; canonically isomorphic
via `φ(a,b) = (a, b*)`. Do not identify components across the two without
routing through φ. Full derivation: `analysis/drive-agda-sketch-vv/port/REFINEMENT-LOG.md`
iteration 13.
